Summary

Bazaar Tales is a single-player puzzle game. Progress is stored on your device only. We do not operate accounts, ads, in-app purchases, or analytics in the current version.

Data we store on your device

The app may save locally (for example via on-device storage):

  • Game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, building upgrades)
  • Sound preferences (music / effects mute)
  • Optional internal sandbox toggle (for testing; not required to play)

This data does not leave your device in the current version. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Data we do not collect

In the current version we do not:

  • Create user accounts or require sign-in
  • Collect name, email, or phone number
  • Show third-party advertisements
  • Offer in-app purchases
  • Use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send personal data to us
  • Use the microphone (audio playback only)
  • Track you across other apps for advertising

Children

The game does not target children with personalized ads or social features. No account is required. If you are a parent and have questions, contact us at the email above.

Third parties

System services provided by Apple or Google (app stores, device OS) may process data under their own policies when you install or update the app. We do not sell personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change (for example if analytics or ads are added later). The “Last updated” date will change; material changes should be reflected in the store listing as required.
